# Huella Local Design System

**Fundación Huella Local** (FHL) is a Chilean foundation that bridges private companies and local governments to fund high-impact infrastructure and equipment projects for municipalities. By mobilizing private sector contributions, FHL enables local governments to unlock public funding they could not otherwise access — closing the equity gap between resourced and under-resourced territories across Chile.

> "Gracias al aporte de empresas, desarrollamos iniciativas de infraestructura y equipamiento, permitiendo que los municipios accedan a financiamiento público para ejecutar obras de alto impacto social."

**Impact (2026):** 948,190 beneficiaries · 276 initiatives · +110M USD public investment · 14× private funding leverage · SDG-aligned (ODS 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 17)

## CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS

### Language & Voice

- **Primarily Spanish** (Chile), with bilingual Spanish/English for international presentations
- **Tone:** Institutional but accessible — confident and data-driven, not cold or bureaucratic
- **Person:** Third person for brand ("En Huella Local trabajamos…"); second person (tú) for audience ("Revisa nuestras propuestas, danos tu opinión")
- **Casing:** Headlines in sentence case or ALL CAPS for section labels/overlines; body in normal sentence case
- **Formality:** Uses **tú** (informal second person), not "usted" — approachable and direct
- **Emoji:** None used in any materials
- **Exclamation:** Used in social content ("¡Feliz día de los trabajadores!"), not in formal presentations
- **Bold emphasis:** Frequently bolds key phrases inline to guide scanning within running text
- **Numbers:** Chilean format — periods as thousands separators: 948.190, not 948,190

### Copywriting Patterns

- Section titles: short, punchy ("DIAGNÓSTICO", "EJES Y MEDIDAS") — uppercase
- Slide captions: sentence case, informative, never marketing-speak
- CTAs: action-first verbs ("Conoce el plan", "Valida el plan aquí")
- Orange highlights: applied to the single most important phrase in a sentence
- Data storytelling: numbers always contextualized ("14x más recursos…")

## VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

### Color System

- **Azul `#7A80A3`** — Muted blue-slate. Primary brand color. Used for headings, plan card numbers, footer stripes on slides, and modals. Professional without being corporate-cold.
- **Naranja `#E28757`** — Warm terra-cotta orange. Primary accent. CTAs, stat highlights, numbered badges, modal underlines, action link hover states. Signals priority and impact.
- **Verde `#8DC899`** — Sage green. Present in the fingerprint logo icon. Associated with sustainability and local growth. Used in the "Franja Verde" slide footer.
- **Gris Texto `#4D4E56`** — Body text, card titles, modal copy.
- **Gris Fondo `#F7F8F9`** — Default page background.
- **White `#FFFFFF`** — Card and slide surfaces.

### Logo Usage

The logotype has three lockups. **It must appear in color or white — not black** (designs too often default to the black logo, which is wrong).

- **Color (`logo-color-v2.svg`) — primary, color sobre blanco:** the default. Use on white and light backgrounds: documents, web headers, slides over light surfaces.
- **Blanco (`logo-blanco-v2.svg`) — blanco sobre color:** use on every colored, photographic or dark background — azul, verde, naranja, franja verde, hero photos. The logo is never set in black over a color.
- **Negro (`logo-negro-v2.svg`) — casi nunca:** only for one-color black-and-white printing, engraving or embroidery. Never the default choice on screen.
- Never recolor the icon, stretch the lockup, or use the icon alone except at very small sizes.

### Typography

- **Font:** Montserrat (Google Fonts), weights 400, 500, 600, 700
  - **Excepción serif:** en afiches de seminario, el titular principal puede ir en **Roboto Serif** (mezclando bold/regular por palabra). Solo el titular; todo lo demás sigue en Montserrat.
  - ⚠️ **Extrabold / black (800) is prohibited.** Bold `700` is the heaviest weight in the system. Use **semibold `600`** or **bold `700`** for titles and headings. The `--font-black` token is deprecated and capped at `700`.
  - ⚠️ *Substitution note: Montserrat is loaded from Google Fonts CDN. For offline or production use, download and self-host the font files. The codebase confirms Montserrat as the single brand typeface.*
- **Display (700):** All-caps, tight tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06–0.12em`) — section titles, hero headings
- **Heading (600–700):** Sentence case, snug line-height — card titles, slide headings
- **Body (400):** Normal tracking, 1.55 line-height — paragraphs, modal content
- **Overlines/labels (700):** All-caps, very wide tracking (`0.08em`) — eje numbers, category badges

### Layout & Spacing

- Max content width: `1100px` with `24px` side padding
- Section vertical padding: `80px`
- Card padding: `24–32px`
- Card grid: 3-column desktop, 2-column tablet, 1-column mobile

#### Outer / edge margins (`tokens/spacing.css`)

**Nothing sits flush to a surface edge** — always reserve the safe gutter for the surface:

- `--margin-xs: 16px` — mobile screens, compact cards
- `--margin-sm: 24px` — web container side padding, tablet
- `--margin-md: 40px` — web section gutters, dense documents
- `--margin-lg: 64px` — document pages, posters
- `--margin-xl: 80px` — generous editorial pages
- `--slide-margin-x: 72px` / `--slide-margin-y: 52px` — 1280×720 slide safe area (content clears the 8px azul stripes)

### Backgrounds & Surfaces

- **Page:** Light gray `#F7F8F9`
- **Cards:** White with subtle border + `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`
- **Hero/Cover:** Full-bleed photography with `rgba(90,95,130,0.6)` azul overlay
- **Glass panels (hero):** `rgba(255,255,255,0.15)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` + subtle white border

### Photography

- **Style:** Real-world photographs of community infrastructure, construction, and civic spaces
- **Treatment:** Converted to grayscale / desaturated, then overlaid with azul `rgba(90,95,130,0.6)` for a unified blue-slate tone
- **Subjects:** Ground-breaking ceremonies, infrastructure sites, community meetings, municipal spaces
- **NOT** stock photography — always documentary, people-centric

### Cards

- White background
- `border-radius: 10px` (`--radius-md`)
- `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)` (`--shadow-sm`) at rest
- `border: 1px solid rgba(122,128,163,0.18)` (`--color-border`)
- Hover: shadow elevates to `0 6px 20px rgba(122,128,163,0.22)` (`--shadow-azul`)
- **No** colored left-border accent on cards

### Buttons

- `border-radius: 10px` (`--radius-md`)
- Font: Montserrat 700, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (`0.07em`)
- Primary: naranja fill, white text, naranja glow shadow
- Hover: `translateY(-1px)` + slightly darker fill

### Slide Design System

- **Thin accent stripes:** `~8px` azul stripe at very top AND very bottom of each slide
- **"Franja Verde":** Full-width sage-green footer bar with white logo + tagline "IMPULSAMOS EL DESARROLLO LOCAL"
- **Cover slides:** Full-bleed photography + azul overlay + white large text
- **Content slides:** White background, naranja/azul text, left vertical orange bar on section headings
- **Bilingual layout:** Spanish bold, English in italic naranja below
- ⚠️ **Prohibited:** small-caps/uppercase tag pills above headings (e.g. an orange rectangle reading "Fundación Huella Local"). No section-label/eyebrow pill component exists in this system — do not reintroduce one.

### Interaction & Animation

- Card hover: shadow elevation only (no transform, no color shift on card body)
- Button hover: `translateY(-1px)` + slightly darker fill color
- Action links hover: azul → naranja color shift
- All transitions: `0.25s ease` (`--transition-base`)
- No decorative animations or loading animations

### Borders & Dividers

- Card borders: `1px solid rgba(122,128,163,0.18)` — very subtle
- Modal heading underline: `2px solid naranja`
- EjeCard action link separator: `1px solid rgba(122,128,163,0.25)` top border
- Section dividers: spacing alone; no horizontal rules

### Corner Radii

- Buttons, cards: `10px`
- Modals, large panels: `16px`
- Badges, pills: `9999px` (full)
- Section label pill: `4px` (slightly rounded rectangle)

## ICONOGRAPHY

### System icons — Google Material Symbols
Huella Local uses **Google Material Symbols (Outlined)** as its icon system. Loaded from Google Fonts CDN. Available via the `.icon` utility class defined in `tokens/icons.css`.

```html
<span class="icon">water_drop</span>
<span class="icon icon-lg">foundation</span>
<span class="icon icon-filled" style="color:var(--color-accent)">eco</span>
```

Recommended icons by context:
- **Agua / hídrico:** `water_drop`, `flood`, `waves`
- **Infraestructura:** `foundation`, `construction`, `location_city`, `account_balance`
- **Comunidad:** `groups`, `handshake`, `volunteer_activism`
- **Medioambiente:** `park`, `eco`, `forest`, `storm`, `thermostat`
- **Energía:** `solar_power`, `bolt`, `energy_savings_leaf`
- **Salud:** `local_hospital`, `health_and_safety`
- **Educación:** `school`, `menu_book`
- **Navegación:** `arrow_forward`, `open_in_new`, `expand_more`

### Ilustraciones de marca
- **Chile Panorama** (`assets/illustrations/chile-panorama.png`): Ilustración panorámica a color de la geografía de Chile — norte desértico, Santiago, sur, Patagonia. Elemento footer de uso **obligatorio** a sangre en documentos formales y presentaciones de cierre. Nunca recortar. Nunca usar como fondo con texto encima.
- **Line art comunal**: Ilustraciones pen-and-ink de ciudades específicas (ej. Vallenar). Se encargan por proyecto. Subir a `assets/illustrations/<ciudad>.png` cuando estén disponibles.

### Íconos PACCC (legado)
7 íconos PNG de amenazas climáticas en `assets/paccc/icono1–7.png`. Estilo ilustrado, bloque de color. Específicos de la plataforma PACCC.

### Reglas
- **Sin emoji** en ningún material
- Flechas direccionales: texto plano `→` en naranja, o ícono `arrow_forward` de Material Symbols
- No dibujar SVG ad-hoc — usar Material Symbols o assets existentes
